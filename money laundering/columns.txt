MATHEMATICAL NOTATION AND DEFINITIONS

t = Timestamp of the current transaction
Orig = Originator account
Dest = Destination account
w = Time window (1h, 24h, or 7d)
Lifespan = Hours since the account's very first transaction in that specific role

RAW DATA AND METADATA
type: The type of transaction (Transfer, Cash-out, etc.)
category: The purpose of the transaction
orig: ID number for the sender
dest: ID number for the receiver
old_balance: Sender's balance before the transaction
new_balance: Sender's balance after the transaction
is_fraud: 1 if fraud, 0 if not
payment_method: How the payment was made
merchant_info.merchant_id: ID for the merchant
merchant_info.category: Merchant industry category
merchant_info.risk_level: Risk score for the merchant
merchant_info.avg_transaction: Merchant's normal transaction size
datetime: Timestamp of the transaction
city_state: Location of the transaction
postcode: Postal code of the transaction
device: Device used
ip_subnet: Network routing identifier

TEMPORAL ENCODINGS (Cyclical time features)
hour_sin: sin(2 * pi * hour / 24)
hour_cos: cos(2 * pi * hour / 24)
day_sin: sin(2 * pi * day / 7)
day_cos: cos(2 * pi * day / 7)
month_sin: sin(2 * pi * month / 12)
month_cos: cos(2 * pi * month / 12)

CORE AMOUNT
amount: The dollar amount of the current transaction

ROLLING WINDOW METRICS (Calculated for w = 1h, 24h, 7d)

Rolling Counts:
orig_count_as_orig_w: Number of prior transactions sent by Orig in the past w window
orig_count_as_dest_w: Number of prior transactions received by Orig in the past w window
dest_count_as_orig_w: Number of prior transactions sent by Dest in the past w window
dest_count_as_dest_w: Number of prior transactions received by Dest in the past w window

Rolling Means (Averages):
orig_mean_amt_as_orig_w: Average amount sent by Orig in the past w window
orig_mean_amt_as_dest_w: Average amount received by Orig in the past w window
dest_mean_amt_as_orig_w: Average amount sent by Dest in the past w window
dest_mean_amt_as_dest_w: Average amount received by Dest in the past w window

Rolling Fan-out (Unique Destinations):
orig_unique_dest_w: Number of unique accounts Orig sent money to in the past w window
dest_unique_dest_w: Number of unique accounts Dest sent money to in the past w window

Rolling Fan-in (Unique Originators):
orig_unique_orig_w: Number of unique accounts Orig received money from in the past w window
dest_unique_orig_w: Number of unique accounts Dest received money from in the past w window

ALL-TIME HISTORICAL BASELINES

All-Time Counts:
orig_all_time_count_as_orig: Total transactions sent by Orig since account creation
orig_all_time_count_as_dest: Total transactions received by Orig since account creation
dest_all_time_count_as_orig: Total transactions sent by Dest since account creation
dest_all_time_count_as_dest: Total transactions received by Dest since account creation

Usual Amount Per Transaction:
orig_usual_amt_as_orig: Total amount sent by Orig / Total transactions sent by Orig
orig_usual_amt_as_dest: Total amount received by Orig / Total transactions received by Orig
dest_usual_amt_as_orig: Total amount sent by Dest / Total transactions sent by Dest
dest_usual_amt_as_dest: Total amount received by Dest / Total transactions received by Dest

Usual Transaction Velocity (Counts per Hour):
orig_usual_count_per_hour_as_orig: Total transactions sent by Orig / Orig's lifespan in hours
orig_usual_count_per_hour_as_dest: Total transactions received by Orig / Orig's lifespan in hours
dest_usual_count_per_hour_as_orig: Total transactions sent by Dest / Dest's lifespan in hours
dest_usual_count_per_hour_as_dest: Total transactions received by Dest / Dest's lifespan in hours

Usual Volume Velocity (Dollars per Hour):
orig_usual_volume_per_hour_as_orig: Total amount sent by Orig / Orig's lifespan in hours
orig_usual_volume_per_hour_as_dest: Total amount received by Orig / Orig's lifespan in hours
dest_usual_volume_per_hour_as_orig: Total amount sent by Dest / Dest's lifespan in hours
dest_usual_volume_per_hour_as_dest: Total amount received by Dest / Dest's lifespan in hours

TEMPORAL STATE METRICS

orig_time_since_last_tx_as_orig: Seconds since Orig's last transaction as a sender
orig_time_since_last_tx_as_dest: Seconds since Orig's last transaction as a receiver
dest_time_since_last_tx_as_orig: Seconds since Dest's last transaction as a sender
dest_time_since_last_tx_as_dest: Seconds since Dest's last transaction as a receiver

PAIRWISE EDGES
pair_count_forward: Total number of past transactions sent from this Orig to this Dest
pair_count_reverse: Total number of past transactions sent from this Dest back to this Orig
